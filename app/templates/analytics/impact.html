Calculation Methodology & Standards

Meal Estimation Formula:
In accordance with USDA / Feeding America and FAO guidelines, one average individual meal is standardized at 400 grams (0.4 kg). When food quantities are entered in kilograms, meals are estimated as quantity × 2.5. When entered as individual servings or plates, a 1:1 conversion is maintained.
Greenhouse Gas (CO2e) Mitigation:
Organic waste in anaerobic landfills decomposes to produce potent methane (CH4) gas. Based on EPA WARM model factors, each kilogram of surplus food diverted from landfills prevents approximately 2.5 kg of CO2 equivalent emissions.
