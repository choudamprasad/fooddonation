How FoodBridge Works

We make donating and receiving surplus food effortless, safe, and transparent in three simple steps.

1

Donors List Surplus Food

Restaurants, hotels, catered events, and colleges publish available food with exact quantity, dietary tags, hygiene details, and safe pickup deadlines.

2

Smart Matching & Request

Our explainable algorithm evaluates proximity, urgency, and recipient capacity to alert nearby verified charities, orphanages, and community kitchens.

3

Verified Pickup & Tracking

The donor accepts the request, coordinates safe transport, confirms handoff, and both parties track the completed distribution impact in real-time.
