Our Core Mission

Bridging Surplus Food with Communities in Need

A technological solution solving a pressing logistical challenge: rapid, safe redistribution of wholesome surplus food.

The Problem We Solve

Globally, over one-third of all food produced is wasted, generating approximately 8-10% of global greenhouse gas emissions. Simultaneously, millions of people experience acute food insecurity daily. Commercial kitchens, banquet halls, hotels, and college dining facilities often have dozens of wholesome, freshly prepared meals remaining at the end of service. Due to a lack of rapid discovery and transparent coordination mechanisms, this food is frequently discarded.

FoodBridge establishes an organized, authenticated network connecting local food donors with verified non-profits, homeless shelters, orphanages, and community kitchens. By structuring listings around rigorous food safety windows and automated urgency matching, surplus food is collected and served while perfectly fresh.

Environmental Care

Diverting food from landfills directly cuts dangerous methane emissions.

Community Dignity

Empowering local grassroots charities with reliable nutrition supplies.

Safety Protocols

Rigid temperature declarations and verified organization vetting.

Hackathon Presentation Edition
