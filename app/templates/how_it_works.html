How the FoodBridge Network Operates

A structured, end-to-end workflow designed for rapid collection before food freshness and safety degrade.

01

Surplus Food Discovery & Donor Listing

Commercial restaurants, conference venues, campus dining halls, and banquet caterers frequently prepare large batches of high-quality food that cannot be re-served the next day due to internal menu cycles. The donor creates a listing on FoodBridge specifying exact quantity, dietary classification, storage conditions, preparation time, and a strict safe pickup deadline.

Preparation Timestamp Cold / Hot Holding Protocol Safe Pickup Deadline
02

Explainable Matching & NGO Request

Rather than relying on black-box heuristics, our explainable ranking algorithm scores available donations across 4 transparent pillars: Proximity (35%), Deadline Urgency (30%), Recipient Distribution Capacity (25%), and Food Category Preferences (10%). Nearby verified charities receive instantaneous notifications and submit collection requests specifying their intended distribution.

Haversine Distance Match Capacity Fit Prevent Duplicate Requests
03

Handoff Coordination, Verification & Impact Tracking

Once the donor reviews and accepts the request, private street address details and direct contact phone numbers are released to the accepted charity. The donor packs the food, marks it Ready for Pickup, and the NGO confirms physical collection. Once redistributed, the transaction closes, immutable audit history is recorded, and impact statistics update automatically.
